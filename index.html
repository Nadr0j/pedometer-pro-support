Help for both iPhone versions

My steps are missing or not updating

Open Pedometer Pro and review the requested Apple Health read access and Motion & Fitness access. On iPhone, try Settings → Refresh health data in the app. After reviewing the prompts, you can check read access under iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Pedometer Pro. To check live phone step access, look under Motion & Fitness in Privacy & Security.

Health history and live phone counting use independent permissions. Empty Health results can mean there are no readable records or read access is limited. Live counting uses compatible motion hardware.

Why is my count different from Apple Health or my watch?

Motion readings and Apple Health history update on different schedules. Apple Watch readings can appear on your iPhone once Apple Health synchronization delivers them. Counts and other metrics can differ temporarily while inputs catch up.

How do streaks and rest days work?

Six consecutive goal days earn one rest day. Reach your goal the next day to keep going. Earn another rest day with six more consecutive goal days. You can hold one rest day at a time.

Why does the first load take longer?

The first import reads your available Health history. On later launches, a protected local cache can show saved activity while Health refreshes. The app can rebuild the cache from permitted Health data if iOS clears it.

How do I change units or remove app data?

Open Settings on iPhone to choose miles or kilometers. To remove the app’s ordinary local files and preferences, choose Delete App. Manage the underlying activity records in Apple Health. See privacy choices and deletion for more information.

Version 1.0 on iPhone

What does Past week show?

The Past week summary covers today and the previous six calendar days. Its totals, daily average, progress, and goal days use that same window. Swipe the daily chart to browse earlier recorded steps.

How does changing my goal affect earlier days?

Set your goal in Settings, from 1,000 to 50,000 steps. Version 1.0 compares past activity with your current goal. Previously earned badges stay unlocked.

Version 1.1 on iPhone and Apple Watch

How do I use the charts on iPhone?

In Steps, tap W, M, or Y to switch views. The ring stays at the top and shows today’s steps, distance, floors climbed, and active calories. Swipe Week or Month to browse daily bars. If you browse away from today, tap the return arrow or tap the Steps tab again.

Year plots daily readings around a semicircle. Each bar’s color shifts as it reaches higher counts. Use the chevrons beside the year to move to another available year. The charts show recorded readings and use the goal targets for those dates.

How do Weekday plan and Today only work?

Under Settings → Daily goals, use Weekday plan to choose the same target each day or a different target by weekday. Plan changes begin tomorrow. Use Today only to adjust the current date. If the date changes while the editor is open, go back and review the new day before saving.

How are older goals, streaks, and badges counted?

Completed dates retain their saved targets. Activity recorded before the goal plan began is compared with the starting goal saved when you adopted it. The original target for an earlier date is unknown.

A readable zero is a below-goal day. A date without readable steps stays unresolved. Health can later reconcile those dates, so you may see a dated last-known streak or a verified minimum. Open Badges to see your streak and milestones, including yearly milestones through ten years. Previously earned badges stay unlocked.

Why are distance, stairs, or active calories missing?

Each metric depends on its own device readings and Health access. Review Steps, Walking + Running Distance, Flights Climbed, and Active Energy permissions. On iPhone a dash means the reading is unavailable. When the Watch has readable activity for today and no floors are recorded, it displays zero stairs.

How do I get started on Apple Watch?

Install Pedometer Pro on a compatible paired Apple Watch and open it. Tap Connect Health when shown, then review the requested read access. Where available, Motion & Fitness can provide live counts while the Watch app is open. Apple Health provides the history and available metrics.

Set your goal plan and distance unit on iPhone. The paired Watch receives those settings through Apple’s WatchConnectivity service. If the Watch shows an older setting, open both apps and choose Settings & Health → Sync preferences on the Watch. You can choose Refresh Health in the same section to read current activity.

How do I add the Daily steps widget?

Open the Watch app once so it can read your activity. From the Smart Stack widget gallery, choose Daily steps. You can also add Pedometer Pro to a compatible complication slot on your watch face. Tap the widget or complication to open the app.

The widget uses a saved daily reading from the Watch app. watchOS manages when it refreshes. If the reading is missing or out of date, unlock the Watch and open Pedometer Pro.
